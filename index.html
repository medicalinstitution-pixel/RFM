Distinctive Strengths

Why RFM Residency is the RIGHT Choice

We have designed the Retama Family Medicine training program from the ground up to address what modern medical graduates genuinely need: balanced volume, rigorous mentorship, zero out-of-town commuting, and continuous community engagement.

True Hybrid Training Model

Unlike programs isolated inside academic ivory towers or small standalone clinics, our residents rotate through both major hospital systems (University Health) and robust community networks (CommuniCare).

100% Local Rotations

All core inpatient, outpatient, and specialty rotations occur within San Antonio. Residents are never asked to disrupt family life for distant rotations or endure long commutes between health systems.

Graduated Continuity Panel

Your panel targets expand steadily from 4 patients per session in PGY-1 to 10 in PGY-3. This structured volume curve prevents burnout while developing swift, confident ambulatory efficiency.

Holistic Resident Wellness

We protect mental and physical health with structured fatigue mitigation, monthly peer support debriefs, quarterly wellness half-days, and a culture that treats sleep, family, and personal dignity as vital.

Dedicated Scholarly Inquiry

Residents receive faculty mentorship to complete two scholarly activities, including at least one Quality Improvement initiative presented at local, regional, or national venues.

Community Advocacy & Equity

San Antonio has a vibrant, bilingual, and medically underserved community. You will train directly on addressing the social determinants of health, language barriers, and healthcare equity.

The Heart of Healthcare

Why Choose Family Medicine?

Family medicine is the cornerstone of a functional healthcare system. It is the only specialty offering true lifelong continuity, where physicians manage undifferentiated acute illnesses, complex multisystem chronic conditions, procedural interventions, and behavioral health across generations.

Cradle-to-Grave Continuity

From welcoming a newborn into the world to supporting elderly patients with dignity and palliative comfort, family physicians provide enduring healthcare partnerships across all ages and life transitions.

Master Diagnostic Reasoning

Family physicians are diagnosticians of the first contact. You evaluate symptoms before they have a label, discerning when to reassure, when to treat in the clinic, and when to escalate to tertiary critical care.

Broad Procedural Breadth

Family doctors perform joint injections, POCUS, skin biopsies, laceration repairs, IUD/Nexplanon placements, and uncomplicated deliveries, delivering full-scope care under one roof.

Unparalleled Career Flexibility

Whether your ultimate passion is outpatient primary care, inpatient hospitalist medicine, emergency care, sports medicine, or public health leadership, family medicine provides the platform to define your own career path.

"Family physicians advocate for all patients and work to remove barriers to care for all populations. They advocate for their patients through the development and promotion of health policy by working with local organizations and partnering to promote better health."

Our Identity & Mission

About Retama Family Medicine

Retama Family Medicine Residency is a new, forward-thinking residency program located in vibrant San Antonio, Texas. Sponsored by TIGMER (Texas Institute for Graduate Medical Education and Research), our program was founded to meet the healthcare needs of South Texas through community-grounded medical training.

Through strategic collaboration with University Health and CommuniCare Health Centers, we combine high-acuity tertiary inpatient care with compassionate, community-based ambulatory medicine.

By fostering a culture of lifelong learning, holistic wellness, and collaborative innovation, our faculty prepare residents to deliver high-quality, evidence-based care that enhances the health and vitality of the diverse families we are privileged to serve.

Core Values

  • Community First: Centering clinical education on the needs of underserved and bilingual patient populations.
  • Clinical Integrity: Practicing high-value, evidence-based, transparent, and patient-centered medicine.
  • Master Adaptive Learning: Developing habit patterns of critical appraisal, continuous self-evaluation, and intellectual curiosity.
  • Holistic Wellness: Prioritizing resident longevity, team camaraderie, psychological safety, and fatigue mitigation.

The Continuity Clinic (FMP) Architecture

The Family Medicine Practice (FMP) serves as the academic home for all residents throughout their 3-year tenure. In accordance with ACGME standards, continuity clinics are maintained for at least 40 weeks per year, guaranteeing longitudinal patient-physician relationships.

Competency-Based Education

Goals and Educational Objectives

Our curriculum adheres rigorously to the ACGME Core Competencies, ensuring that graduating residents possess the knowledge, surgical and procedural capabilities, and ethical foundation required for independent, autonomous family practice.

Patient Care & Procedural Skills

  • Provide whole-person, family-centered, community-focused continuous care across the human lifespan.
  • Demonstrate independent management of undifferentiated ambulatory complaints and acutely hospitalized adults and children.
  • Perform essential procedures: spontaneous vaginal deliveries, POCUS, joint injections, suturing, and minor dermatologic surgeries.

Medical Knowledge

  • Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of biomedical, clinical, and epidemiological sciences relevant to primary care.
  • Understand prior probability and test performance characteristics to order and interpret diagnostic testing judiciously.
  • Formulate evidence-based treatment plans tailored to patient social determinants of health and economic circumstances.

Practice-Based Learning & Improvement

  • Systematically analyze practice panels using clinical quality indicators and patient outcomes to optimize care delivery.
  • Design and execute interdisciplinary Quality Improvement (QI) and patient safety projects.
  • Formulate targeted clinical questions and appraise medical literature critically for real-world applicability and bias.

Systems-Based Practice & Leadership

  • Navigate complex healthcare systems, care coordination networks, insurance models, and community support agencies.
  • Lead interprofessional clinical teams (nursing, pharmacy, social work, medical assistants) effectively.
  • Advocate for health equity, eliminate care disparities, and practice high-value, cost-conscious medical stewardship.
Clinical Curriculum

Residency Rotations

Comprehensive hands-on training structured across inpatient tertiary services and longitudinal community settings.

Inpatient Adult Medicine

600 Hours • 750+ Encounters

Rigorous adult inpatient medicine services at University Health. Residents diagnose complex multisystem conditions, manage acute resuscitations, and lead inpatient discharge transitions.

Critical Care / ICU

PGY-2 & PGY-3 Intensive

Management of critically ill patients, mechanical ventilation, vasopressor titration, arterial lines, central lines, and complex family prognostic conferences.

Obstetrics & Maternal Care

200 Hours • 20+ Deliveries

Labor and delivery suite management, triage, spontaneous vaginal deliveries, recognition of obstetric emergencies, postpartum care, and newborn transitions.

Pediatrics (Inpatient & Ambulatory)

PGY-1 & PGY-2

Comprehensive infant, child, and adolescent care. Newborn nursery evaluations, inpatient pediatric service, ambulatory well-child checks, PALS, and NRP training.

Emergency Medicine

100 Hours • 125+ Encounters

Dedicated emergency department shifts caring for acutely ill and injured patients, airway stabilization, rapid triage, wound closure, and procedural sedation.

Musculoskeletal & Sports Medicine

Ambulatory & Inpatient

Outpatient orthopedics, acute joint injuries, sports physicals, fracture care, joint aspirations/injections, casting/splinting, and physical therapy coordination.

Geriatrics & Palliative Medicine

100 Hours • 125+ Encounters

Comprehensive assessment of older adults across hospital, clinic, and skilled nursing facilities, focusing on polypharmacy, fall prevention, cognitive screening, and goals of care.

General Surgery

PGY-1 Experience

Surgical triage, preoperative clearance, perioperative fluid/electrolyte optimization, post-op care, and assisting with core surgical procedures.

Health System Management (HSM)

PGY-2 Dedicated

Clinical practice operations, billing and coding compliance, healthcare economics, value-based care contracts, population health metrics, and clinical leadership.

Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS)

Integrative & Lifestyle Medicine

Addiction Medicine

Outpatient Psychiatry

Global & Border Health

Protected Academic Didactics

Education is safeguarded at Retama Family Medicine. Every Thursday afternoon is 100% protected from all routine inpatient and outpatient duties, allowing residents and faculty to gather for interactive, evidence-based learning.

Core Clinical Curriculum

System-based modular lectures reviewing current American Board of Family Medicine (ABFM) guidelines, acute and chronic disease management, pharmacology pearls, and outpatient clinical pearls.

Hands-On Simulation & Skills

Quarterly procedural workshops covering Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS), joint injections, punch/shave biopsies, Nexplanon insertion and removal, IUD placement, and advanced laceration repair.

Journal Club & Evidence Analysis

Regularly scheduled critical appraisal sessions. Residents dissect recent RCTs, evaluate study methodology for bias, and assess how findings can be applied directly to South Texas patient care.

Applicant Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have graduated from one of the following to be considered for an interview and ranking:

  • Graduates of medical schools in the US and Canada accredited by the Liaison Committee on Medical Education (LCME).
  • Graduates of colleges of osteopathic medicine in the US accredited by the American Osteopathic Association (AOA).
  • Graduates of medical schools outside the US who have completed a Fifth Pathway program provided by an LCME-accredited medical school.
  • International medical graduates (IMGs) with a currently valid certificate from the Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG) or a full and unrestricted US medical license.
Important Policies: Date of graduation must be no more than 5 years from the date of application in ERAS. All applicants MUST be U.S. citizens or permanent residents. We do not sponsor H-1B visas.

Required Documentation & Examination Requirements

Required Documents in ERAS

  • Complete ERAS Application & Personal Statement
  • Medical Student Performance Evaluation (MSPE / Dean's Letter)
  • At least two (2) Letters of Recommendation (LORs)
  • Official Medical School Transcript
  • Valid ECFMG Status Report (if applicable)

Board Examination Minimums

NBOME (COMLEX)

Minimum passing score of 400 for COMLEX Level 1 and Level 2-CE. Must take and pass Level 2-PE (we will still consider applicants who plan to retake or have not yet taken Level 2-PE).

USMLE

Minimum passing score of 194 for Step 1 and 209 for Step 2 CK (we will still consider applicants who plan to retake or have Step 2 CK scheduled).
